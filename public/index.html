    What this lab models

    A digital workbench.
    Not a physical circuit solver.

    Logic & timing
    VHDL compiled and executed by the installed GHDL engine, connected through a native VPI bridge. Nine-state logic, native assertions, event timing, ideal clock stimulus and native VCD capture. The website itself is not a certified verification tool.
    Board visualization
    Photo-referenced component layout, active-low keys and LEDs, and multiplexed display persistence. The model is illustrative, not a dimensionally certified CAD model.
    Not simulated
    Voltage, current, power consumption, analog transients, signal integrity, metastability, thermal behavior, or FPGA place-and-route timing. Peripheral chips are visual references, not complete electronic models.
    Files & projects
    VHD/VHDL and VHO netlists compile locally; vendor netlists require their matching VHDL primitive libraries. CF files are GHDL indexes: include their source files and set the correct libraries. QSF imports pin assignments, not a Quartus build. SDF requires matching VITAL timing models and an instance path. HEX/MIF/MEM/TXT/DAT files are provided to the testbench as data; they do not configure the FPGA. VCD replays digital and real traces with selectable board scope. Verilog/SystemVerilog and proprietary bitstreams cannot execute in this GHDL installation.
    Resource limits
    Four simultaneous simulations, 100 source/data files, 512 observed native signals, 4096 bits per signal, 16 hierarchy levels. Native waveform capture stops at 64 MB; replay accepts 32 MB and 250,000 timestamps. Waveforms reject times outside the exact integer range rather than silently rounding. The analyzer retains recent changes; native VCD retains the captured history.
    Local execution
    Run only trusted HDL. GHDL testbenches execute locally and may access files; this workbench is not a security sandbox. Projects autosave in this browser and can be exported as JSON; simulation processes and waveform history are not restored after closing the page.
    Physical hardware
    No JTAG connection. SOF/POF files are inspected, never “programmed.” Verify pin assignments and I/O standards against your exact board schematic before using real hardware.
